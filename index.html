01 · Lights

Any color you like.

Pick the lightbar's color and brightness, or let it cycle. Choose which player LEDs light up, and turn the microphone LED on or let it pulse.

02 · Adaptive triggers

Feel it while you set it.

Give L2 and R2 a steady resistance, a click like a gun trigger, the pull of a bow, or a ramp that grows as you press. A graph follows the real trigger as you try it.

  • Ready-made effects and a custom one with handles

Test both motors.

Play a pulse, a heartbeat, a ramp or a steady hum on the left motor, the right one or both, and see it spread from the handles on screen.

Questions

Can I use a PS5 controller on a Mac?

Yes. macOS supports the DualSense over Bluetooth and USB, and games use it like any controller. What macOS does not offer is a way to change its lightbar, its adaptive triggers or its LEDs: that is what DS5Mac adds.

How do I connect a DualSense to my Mac?

Over Bluetooth: hold the PS and Create buttons until the lightbar flashes, then choose DualSense Wireless Controller in System Settings › Bluetooth. Over USB: plug in a USB-C cable.

Do my settings stay on while I play?

Yes. DS5Mac keeps applying them in the background, with the game in front. A game that sets the lights or the triggers itself wins while it does.

Can DS5Mac change the vibration of my games?

No. macOS does not let another app change what a game sends to the motors. DS5Mac tests both motors and sets the strength of its own effects.

Is DS5Mac free? Does it track me?

It is free, with no account, no ads and no analytics. Its only connection is a daily check for updates, which sends no personal data.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Macs with Apple silicon or Intel.

How do I uninstall DS5Mac?

Quit it from its menu bar panel and move it from Applications to the Trash. Its settings are in the folder Library/Containers/com.imellag.DS5Mac in your home folder.
